I sat down to write this and realized I don't actually know how to say what I want to say, which is probably the truest thing I can tell you. I've watched you carry whole weeks on your shoulders and still come home soft. That's not nothing. Most people get hard. You didn't.

You make ordinary days feel like they're keeping a secret. The way you laugh at your own jokes before the punchline. The way you fall asleep mid-sentence and I have to finish the thought for you. I love that. I love you. Not in the clean, tidy way songs make it sound. In the messy way, where I forget what I was saying because you walked into the room.

I'm not good at this. I rehearsed three different versions of this letter and they all sounded like a greeting card. So here's the version where I just tell you the truth: I'm proud of you. I'm proud of who you are when nobody's watching. I'm proud of the way you keep showing up even when the week has been long and the world has been loud.

You are my favorite person. Not in a small way. In the way where every plan I make starts with what would lizzie think of this. I don't want to be anywhere you're not. I don't want to imagine a version of my life that doesn't have you in it, eating cereal at midnight, stealing my hoodie, calling me honeybee back like it's the most obvious thing in the world.

So happy birthday, lizzie. Make a wish. I already made mine and it's you. It's always been you.
